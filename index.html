Day-by-Day Itinerary

Landing in Sri Lanka and meeting your private chauffeur. Transfer to Negombo for check-in and relaxation after your flight.

🤝 Airport Meet & Greet 🚗 Private Transfer 🌊 Negombo Relaxation
Overnight: Negombo

Travel from Negombo to Sigiriya. En route, visit the historic Dambulla Cave Temple and the Golden Temple before proceeding to Sigiriya for your stay.

🛕 Dambulla Cave Temple 🛕 Golden Temple
Overnight: Sigiriya

Embark on an exciting Habarana elephant safari, followed by an immersive traditional village tour and an evening cultural show.

🐘 Habarana Elephant Safari 🌾 Village Tour 💃 Culture Show
Overnight: Sigiriya

Morning excursion to climb the magnificent Sigiriya Rock Fortress. In the afternoon, visit the ancient ruins of the Polonnaruwa historical city.

🥾 Sigiriya Rock Fortress 🏛️ Polonnaruwa Visit
Overnight: Sigiriya

Drive towards Kandy with a stop at a Matale Spice Garden en route. In the evening, visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic.

🌶️ Matale Spice Garden 🛕 Temple of the Tooth
Overnight: Kandy

Explore the Royal Botanical Gardens in Peradeniya during the morning. Spend a relaxed afternoon in Kandy.

🌿 Peradeniya Botanical Garden ☕ Afternoon Relaxation
Overnight: Kandy

Scenic drive from Kandy to Nuwara Eliya. En route, visit a working tea factory and plantation, and admire the cascading Ramboda Falls.

☕ Tea Factory & Plantation 🌊 Ramboda Falls
Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

Enjoy the world-renowned scenic train journey from Nuwara Eliya to Ella through rolling green hills and misty mountains.

🚂 Scenic Train Journey
Overnight: Ella

Explore Ella's highlights including a hike up Little Adam's Peak and a visit to the iconic Nine Arch Bridge. Spend the rest of the day relaxing in Ella.

🥾 Little Adam's Peak 🌉 Nine Arch Bridge
Overnight: Ella

Travel from Ella to Yala, stopping along the way to witness the stunning Ravana Falls.

🌊 Ravana Falls 🚗 Scenic Drive
Overnight: Yala

Early morning wildlife safari at Yala National Park. Afterward, travel to the historic city of Galle.

🐆 Yala Morning Safari 🚗 Drive to Galle
Overnight: Galle

Visit a sea turtle hatchery, enjoy a scenic boat ride on Koggala Lagoon, and spend the evening walking around the historic Galle Fort.

🐢 Sea Turtle Hatchery 🚤 Koggala Lagoon Boat Ride 🏰 Galle Fort Walk
Overnight: Galle

Drive from Galle to Colombo. Enjoy a city tour around Colombo and relax for the remainder of the day.

🏙️ Colombo City Tour 🌴 Afternoon Relaxation
Overnight: Colombo

Private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport for your departure flight home.

Departure Transfer: Private airport drop-off.
